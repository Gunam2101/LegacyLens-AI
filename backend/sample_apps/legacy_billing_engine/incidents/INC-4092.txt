INCIDENT POST-MORTEM REPORT: INC-4092
TITLE: Weekend Settlement Grace Period Premature Fee Penalty Defect
SEVERITY: P1 - Production Compliance Breach
DATE: 2026-10-15

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Customers were prematurely assessed late penalty fees during weekend settlement processing. 
Root cause investigation revealed a direct business rule conflict:
1. Source code rule in billing.py (evaluate_grace_period) implements an outdated 3-day grace period threshold.
2. The legally binding corporate policy and change ticket CR-8812 require a 7-day grace period before applying penalty assessments during weekend settlements.
3. Source code must be updated to 7 days to eliminate compliance violation.

ADDITIONAL SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE AUDIT:
The compliance committee confirmed that `audit_compliance_logger.py` is invoked dynamically via cron batch scripts to log audit hashes. 
Under no circumstances should `audit_compliance_logger.py` be removed or considered obsolete, as it is a mandatory compliance requirement.
